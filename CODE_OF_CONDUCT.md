# Code of conduct

Participants must treat contributors respectfully and avoid harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing and disclosure of private information. Critique
ideas and evidence rather than people. Security research must stay within systems
whose owners authorized the work.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive contributions, restrict participation or
ban repeat offenders. Enforcement should be proportionate and consistent.
Report conduct issues privately to repository maintainers through an available
private channel; do not expose affected people's details in public reports.

This concise project policy draws on the principles of Contributor Covenant;
it is not a verbatim copy or a claim of formal adoption of its full text.
